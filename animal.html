ABOUT THE BOOK

What might the world be like from another animal’s perspective?

An exploration of consciousness beyond the human perspective. This book considers evolutionary continuity, animal perception, emotional life, social behavior, communication, and intelligence, asking how people can approach experiences they cannot directly share.

From observations in the wild to human-animal relationships, the discussion connects questions about animal minds with welfare, conservation, and ethical responsibility. It invites readers to reconsider familiar assumptions while recognizing the challenges of interpreting behavior across species.

THE CENTRAL IDEAS

What you will explore

  • Evolution and comparative cognition
  • Sensory diversity and social emotions
  • Communication and interpretation
  • Animal welfare and conservation
