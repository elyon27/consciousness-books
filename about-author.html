ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Bienuel C. Tenio

Author. Technology practitioner.
A student of consciousness and the human experience.

Bienuel C. Tenio is a Filipino author based in Cebu City whose work brings together an interest in technology, personal development, consciousness, and spiritual reflection. In his seven-book Consciousness collection, he approaches the inner life through several disciplines, inviting readers to consider how scientific questions and philosophical inquiry can inform one another.

Tenio holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering from the University of Cebu. His professional background includes service as an Information Systems Analyst II at the Bureau of Internal Revenue’s Revenue Data Center Visayas, from February 1996 until his resignation took effect in March 2008. This experience in information systems forms part of the technical background he brings to his wider interests in mind, knowledge, and digital tools.

His writing on consciousness moves across neuroscience and artificial intelligence, Eastern and Western thought, dreams, quantum physics, animal experience, and altered states. These subjects are connected by a recurring concern: how people understand awareness, interpret experience, and reflect on their place in the world. Rather than limiting the collection to one explanatory framework, he invites readers to examine the questions from several perspectives.

The collection’s scope reflects an interest in both the measurable and the personal. Brain research and computational systems appear alongside cultural traditions, philosophical debates, and reflections on meaning. Across these books, Tenio encourages curiosity, discernment, and attention to the ethical implications of the ideas being explored.

His broader digital work includes CrownMind Institute and projects concerned with reading, personal growth, and Christian reflection. As a Catholic lay Christian, he regards faith and service as important parts of his life. His educational and devotional interests provide context for his work, while his consciousness books offer exploratory discussions across multiple traditions and fields.

Tenio describes his position as that of a student rather than a teacher. That outlook gives the collection its central invitation: to keep asking questions, to examine assumptions carefully, and to approach the mystery of conscious experience with humility. The books are intended as starting points for thought and conversation, rather than as claims to have resolved the nature of consciousness.
