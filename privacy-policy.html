Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how Consciousness Books, operated by Bienuel C. Tenio in connection with CrownMind Institute, handles information when you browse this website, follow book purchase links, or contact us.

Information collected

The current website presents book information and links to external purchase pages. It does not provide visitor accounts, registration forms, comment forms, or an on-site payment checkout. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message, and any attachments.

Hosting and technical information

The website is intended to be hosted on Netlify. The hosting provider may process technical information, including IP addresses, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs, to deliver the website and maintain its security. Provider practices and retention are governed by their own policies.

Cookies, analytics, and fonts

The current web package contains no site-added analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or visitor cookies. Hosting infrastructure and external services may have their own practices. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts; your browser makes requests to Google when retrieving those fonts, which can disclose technical information such as your IP address and browser request details.

Book purchases through Gumroad

Book purchase buttons take you to Gumroad. Payment, checkout, and digital delivery occur through Gumroad rather than this website. Gumroad and its payment providers handle information under their own privacy policies. Depending on the transaction and seller settings, the author may receive order information, such as a buyer’s email address, purchased product, and transaction details, for order support and administration. This website does not collect or store payment card details.

How information is used

Information you provide may be used to respond to inquiries, assist with purchases, maintain necessary transaction records, and address security or misuse concerns. We do not sell personal information or use inquiry emails to subscribe you to marketing without your permission. Information may be disclosed to service providers when needed for these purposes or where required by applicable law.

Retention and security

We retain correspondence and necessary records only for as long as reasonably needed for their purpose and applicable obligations. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled, but no internet service or email transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Hosting and purchase providers maintain their own retention and security arrangements.

Your choices and requests

You may browse without submitting personal information, choose whether to follow external links, and manage browser privacy settings. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold, or to raise a privacy concern, email contact@crownmind.net. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate purposes or applicable obligations. Requests concerning information held independently by Gumroad, Netlify, or Google should also be directed to that provider.

Children’s privacy

The website does not offer features designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and address it.

External websites

Links to Gumroad, CrownMind Institute, and other external websites are subject to those websites’ policies. Please review their privacy information before providing personal information.

Policy updates and contact

We may update this policy as website features or practices change. The updated policy and revision date will appear on this page.
