ABOUT THE BOOK

What changes when our ordinary state of awareness changes?

A broad survey of experiences that depart from ordinary waking awareness. The book brings together historical and cultural perspectives, neuroscience, meditation, dreams, psychedelic research, shamanic traditions, and technologies that change perception.

Its final chapters turn toward interpretation, integration, informed consent, cultural respect, and the responsibilities involved in studying altered experience. The emphasis is on exploring how changes in awareness raise questions about perception, reality, and the way people understand themselves.

THE CENTRAL IDEAS

What you will explore

  • Ordinary and altered awareness
  • Meditation, dreams, and cultural traditions
  • Technology and perception
  • Consent, integration, and research ethics

INSIDE THE BOOK

A chapter-by-chapter guide

Chapter titles follow the supplied edition; descriptions summarize their subjects.

  1. Introduction to Consciousness and Altered States

    Defines the scope of consciousness and altered experience.

  2. Historical Perspectives on Altered States

    Surveys cultural and historical contexts for changes in awareness.

  3. The Neuroscience of Consciousness

    Introduces the brain processes considered in the study of altered states.

  4. Meditation and Mindfulness

    Examines contemplative practices and changes in attention and awareness.

  5. Psychedelics and Altered Consciousness

    Discusses historical use and research questions surrounding psychoactive substances.

  6. Dreams and Lucid Dreaming

    Considers dream experience and awareness within dreams.

  7. Altered States in Shamanic Traditions

    Explores the cultural and spiritual contexts of traditional practices.

  8. Technological Alterations of Consciousness

    Discusses virtual reality and brain-computer interfaces.

  9. The Philosophy of Consciousness

    Examines metaphysical questions about perception, experience, and reality.

  10. Integration and Future Directions

    Considers ethical responsibility, cultural sensitivity, and the interpretation of experiences.

READING THIS VOLUME

Who it is for

Readers seeking a comparative overview of altered experience across science, culture, and philosophy.

A thoughtful reading approach

Read this volume as an educational and philosophical exploration. Distinguish observations, interpretations, and hypotheses as you follow the discussion.

Descriptions of psychoactive substances and traditional practices are contextual discussions, rather than instructions for use or treatment recommendations.
