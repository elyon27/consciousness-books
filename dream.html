ABOUT THE BOOK

What can dreams reveal about the mind that experiences them?

A multidisciplinary exploration of dreaming through psychology, neuroscience, culture, and personal reflection. The book introduces dreams as a distinctive mode of experience and follows the changing ways people have interpreted their nighttime narratives.

Historical accounts and the ideas of Freud and Jung sit alongside discussions of sleep, dream content, lucid dreaming, creativity, and self-discovery. The book also raises questions about interpretation, the privacy of inner experience, and the ethical implications of technologies that might influence dreams.

THE CENTRAL IDEAS
