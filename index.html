What does it mean
to be conscious?

Awareness is intimate and familiar, yet its nature remains a profound question. This collection approaches that question through brain science, artificial intelligence, cultural traditions, dreams, physics, animal life, and altered states.

Read in the order below for a journey from biological foundations to wider questions of experience—or begin with the subject that sparks your curiosity. Each volume offers a different lens.
